How your projection works ＋

Annual projections through age 100 use constant returns. Growth is applied before year-end contributions or withdrawals. Annual savings are split in proportion to current balances, or equally when all are zero. Withdrawals are taken proportionally. Expenses and passive income stay at the annual amounts you enter. Social Security stays at the monthly amount you enter. Social Security starts at the selected age. Current annual income and living expenses show how much is available to invest. Only the annual amount you choose to invest is added to savings; retirement expenses are entered separately.

Home and cars aren’t sold. Debts, market volatility, taxes on investment gains or withdrawals, fees, and account access restrictions are not modeled. Early 401(k) withdrawals may have restrictions or penalties. Enter after-tax passive income and Social Security estimates. The income chart shows the withdrawals needed to meet your spending goal, not guaranteed sustainable income. Projections are illustrative, not financial advice.
